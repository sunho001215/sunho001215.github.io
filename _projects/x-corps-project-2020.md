## Project Description

This project was supported by the Practical Problem Research Group at Seoul National University, which aims to cultivate talent capable of solving real-world problems by supporting interdisciplinary research led primarily by undergraduate students.

## Details

The theme and content of this project are identical to those of the [K-Startup Maker Project 2020](/projects/k-startup-maker-project-2020/).

## Remarks

This project was carried out with a grant of $5,000, and our team received the Excellence Prize along with $1,000 in prize money. The entire project was carried out under the guidance of Prof. [Songhwai Oh](https://rllab.snu.ac.kr/people/songhwai-oh) at Seoul National University.
