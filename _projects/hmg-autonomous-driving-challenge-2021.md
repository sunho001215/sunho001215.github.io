## Project Description

This competition is organized by Hyundai Motor Group and is open to both undergraduate and graduate students in Korea. The preliminary round takes place in the CarMaker simulation environment, where teams complete tasks such as dynamic and static obstacle avoidance, traffic light handling, and lane keeping. The final round features similar missions on modified real vehicles.

## Details

I developed software to complete the missions in the CarMaker simulation, including object detection with a modified YOLOv3, object tracking with a Kalman filter using LiDAR and camera data, and path planning with an optimal Frenet planner.

## Remarks

After passing the document screening, our team competed through the preliminary round. The entire project was carried out under the guidance of Prof. Kyongsu Yi at Seoul National University.
