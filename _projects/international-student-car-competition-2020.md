## Project Description

This undergraduate competition is organized by the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport of South Korea. It includes tasks such as static and dynamic obstacle avoidance, traffic light recognition, parking, and curve driving, and awards go to the teams that complete all missions in the shortest time. The competition covers everything from building an autonomous driving platform to developing the autonomous driving software itself.

## Details

I served as deputy leader of the entire team and, at the same time, as leader of the vision team. The project was conducted at the Future Mobility Technology Center (FMTC) at Seoul National University. Our team built an autonomous driving platform with two cameras, one LiDAR, one GPS, and one IMU, and developed a complete autonomous driving stack covering HD map creation, localization, path planning, object detection, and vision algorithms. My own work focused on real-time parking slot detection with a modified YOLOv3, traffic light detection with YOLOv3, and lane detection with LaneNet. ([GitHub](https://github.com/snuzero2020/zero))

## Remarks
