## Project Description

This project is funded by the Korea Institute for Advancement of Technology (KIAT). It is a collaborative research initiative among KIAT, Georgia Tech, ETRI, and Neuromeka.

## Details

I am the lead developer of imitation learning algorithms for single-arm and dual-arm humanoid robots, targeting assistive tasks in hospital environments. As part of this project, I worked as a visiting researcher at [Ha Lab](https://faculty.cc.gatech.edu/~sha9/), Georgia Tech, from June to September 2025.
