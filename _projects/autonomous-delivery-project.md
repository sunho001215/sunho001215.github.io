## Project Description

This project was conducted at the [Autonomous Robot Intelligence Lab](https://arisnu.squarespace.com/) (ARI Lab), with the goal of developing a delivery robot that can deliver food without human intervention.

## Details

I participated in the initial phase of the project and contributed to the prototype design.

## Remarks

The entire project was carried out under the guidance of Prof. [Seong-Woo Kim (Sungwoo Kim)](https://arisnu.squarespace.com/director) at Seoul National University.
