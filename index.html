I'm a Ph.D. student in Electrical Engineering at KAIST (Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology), advised by Prof. Min Jun Kim in the Intelligent Robotic Systems Lab (IRSL), where I also completed my M.S. in 2026. I received my B.S. from Seoul National University in Mechanical Engineering and System Semiconductor Engineering for AI.

I am broadly interested in robot learning, with a focus on visuomotor control and manipulation. My current work explores how robots can learn scalable visual representations from images alone—representations that capture enough 3D structure and semantics to support robust manipulation in real-world environments. Ultimately, I hope to contribute to robot foundation models and vision–language–action systems that generalize across tasks, viewpoints, and embodiments.
